### `ev_bargadari_act_1950`

- **West Bengal Bargadars Act, 1950**, s. 3: Division of produce: seed returned to whoever supplied it; owner and bargadar each one-third of the balance; the last third by contribution to cultivation costs; a bargadar entitled by contract or custom to more keeps it.

**Text changed.** The old text said the Act 'formally recognised sharecroppers' entitlement to two-thirds of the produce where they supplied the inputs'. Section 3 sets a one-third/one-third/contribution split instead. The two-thirds wording came from exam-prep sites.

> *title:* West Bengal Bargadars Act passed

> *summaryCard:* In March 1950 the West Bengal Bargadars Act set rules for dividing a sharecropped harvest. It did not grant the two-thirds the movement had demanded: after seed, owner and bargadar each got a third, and the last third was split by what each had put into cultivation.

> *fullEntry:* The West Bengal Bargadars Act, 1950 (West Bengal Act II of 1950) laid down how produce was to be divided where owner and bargadar had no written agreement. Whoever supplied the seed first took back an equal quantity of produce. Owner and bargadar were then each entitled to one-third of the balance, and the remaining third was divided 'in such proportion as would be fair and reasonable', according to each side's contribution of plough-cattle, implements, manure and the cost of protecting or irrigating the land. A bargadar entitled to a larger share by contract or local custom kept it. In practice, a bargadar who supplied all the inputs could reach two-thirds, but the Act did not make two-thirds the rule.

## How to apply

1. On GitHub, open **Actions**, then **Apply source review**, then **Run workflow**. Leave **apply** unticked. This is a dry run: it prints every change and writes nothing.
2. Read the printed changes.
3. In Sanity, create a token: sanity.io/manage, the project, **API**, **Tokens**, **Add API token**, with **Editor** rights. Copy it.
4. On GitHub, open the repository's **Settings**, **Secrets and variables**, **Actions**, **New repository secret**. Name it `SANITY_WRITE_TOKEN` and paste the token.
5. Run **Apply source review** again with **apply** ticked. All changes go in one transaction.
6. Run **Sync content from Sanity**. The site updates about a minute later.

With Node installed, the same can be run locally: `node tools/apply-source-review.js tools/source-review/2026-11.json` for the dry run, and with `SANITY_WRITE_TOKEN` set and `--apply` to write.
